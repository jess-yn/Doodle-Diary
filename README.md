# Doodle Diary

Doodle Diary if for artists, aspiring artists and casual doodlers who want to document their day-to-day life through a sketch (or an elaborate masterpiece if one so chooses), rather than a long-winded essay for those who do not enjoy writing out their thoughts. To help get an idea for a sketch out there are fields which help doodles summarise their mood, recount a moment, and record a quote or motto which they found inspires them.

It's time for us who have struggled to break into the journaling habit, to give it another try but through a different medium.
